# VYNTRA - Terms and Conditions of Use

Version: 2026.08

VYNTRA is a software platform for work-time records, attendance, work activity
and operational evidence on work devices. The customer company determines who
uses the system, for what purpose, how long information is retained and which
authorized users may access it.

VYNTRA is not the employer, supervisor or labor auditor of monitored persons.
The platform does not make disciplinary, compensation, termination or
performance-evaluation decisions. Any employment decision belongs exclusively to
the customer company and requires human review.

Use of VYNTRA requires prior notice to the worker, configuration that is
proportionate to the employment purpose, role-based access controls and
compliance with the law applicable in each country where the customer company
operates.

VYNTRA does not record keystrokes, capture passwords, activate camera or
microphone, open personal files or capture activity outside an active work shift.

The customer company is responsible for obtaining required authorizations,
responding to access, correction, deletion or objection requests, and keeping
privacy and HR contacts up to date.

These terms must be supplemented with the privacy policy, the employee
monitoring notice, the data processing agreement and any applicable local
annexes.
